# Mechanical Engineering Methods

Mechanical Engineering Methods covers the fundamental analytical,
computational, and experimental approaches used to solve engineering
problems. This coursework was about building a **practical toolkit for
mechanical design and analysis** --- from hand calculations and
experimental validation to modern simulation and computational modeling.
It provided the framework I now use to approach engineering challenges
across disciplines.

## What I Learned

-   Applied **analytical methods** such as free-body diagrams, energy
    methods, and dimensional analysis to develop first-principles
    models.\
-   Practiced **computational approaches** using MATLAB, Python, and
    engineering software to solve equations, simulate systems, and
    analyze data.\
-   Conducted **error analysis and uncertainty quantification** in
    experimental work to ensure results were accurate and reliable.\
-   Integrated **numerical methods** (finite difference, root finding,
    optimization techniques) for solving engineering problems beyond
    closed-form solutions.\
-   Gained experience presenting results through **clear technical
    reports, data visualization, and structured problem-solving
    workflows**.

## Core Concepts in Practice

-   **Problem Formulation:** Translating physical situations into
    solvable mathematical models.\
-   **Analytical vs. Numerical Trade-offs:** Knowing when exact
    solutions are possible and when approximation or simulation is
    necessary.\
-   **Data and Error Analysis:** Using statistics, regression, and
    uncertainty quantification to validate results.\
-   **Software as an Extension of Engineering Judgment:** Leveraging
    MATLAB, Python, and Excel not just to compute, but to explore design
    space, optimize, and communicate insights.

## Why It Matters for My Career

Mechanical Engineering Methods provided me with the foundation to
approach **complex, open-ended engineering challenges** systematically.
Future employers can expect me to:

-   **Break down complex problems** into structured models that can be
    analyzed and solved.\
-   **Leverage computational tools** to accelerate design, optimization,
    and validation.\
-   **Critically evaluate results** through uncertainty analysis and
    experimental validation.\
-   **Communicate engineering insights effectively** in written, visual,
    and computational formats.

These skills are universal across mechanical engineering --- whether in
**aerospace, robotics, energy systems, or advanced manufacturing** ---
and ensure that I bring a **methodical, data-driven, and adaptable
approach** to solving engineering problems.
