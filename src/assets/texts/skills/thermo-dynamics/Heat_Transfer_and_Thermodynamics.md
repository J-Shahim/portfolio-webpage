# Heat Transfer and Thermodynamics

Heat Transfer and Thermodynamics provided me with the foundation to
understand how **energy moves through systems** and how that energy can
be harnessed, controlled, and optimized in engineering applications.
This coursework combined fundamental science with practical engineering
tools, giving me the ability to analyze and design systems where heat
and energy transfer are critical.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I Did

-   Applied the **laws of thermodynamics** to closed and open systems.\
-   Calculated **energy balances** involving work, heat, and internal
    energy changes.\
-   Analyzed **conduction heat transfer** through walls, fins, and
    composite systems.\
-   Studied **convection heat transfer**, including natural, forced, and
    mixed convection.\
-   Evaluated **radiative heat transfer**, surface emissivity, and view
    factors in radiative exchange.\
-   Combined conduction, convection, and radiation in **combined-mode
    heat transfer problems**.\
-   Explored **power and refrigeration cycles** such as Rankine,
    Brayton, Otto, and vapor-compression systems.\
-   Performed **efficiency and performance calculations** for engines,
    turbines, and heat exchangers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Concepts in Action

-   **Thermodynamics:** Conservation of energy, entropy generation, and
    cycle efficiency analysis.\
-   **Conduction:** Fourier's law, steady/unsteady heat conduction, and
    thermal resistance networks.\
-   **Convection:** Newton's law of cooling, convective coefficients,
    and heat exchanger design.\
-   **Radiation:** Stefan--Boltzmann law, emissivity, and radiative heat
    transfer between surfaces.\
-   **Heat Exchangers:** Effectiveness-NTU methods for sizing and
    performance evaluation.\
-   **Cycle Analysis:** Applying thermodynamics to power generation,
    propulsion, and refrigeration.
