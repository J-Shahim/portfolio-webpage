# Instrumentation and Measurement

Instrumentation and Measurement taught me how to connect engineering
theory to the physical world through sensors, data acquisition, and
experimental validation. This coursework wasn't just about wiring
circuits --- it was about **building systems that capture, process, and
interpret data**, whether from physical sensors or digital images.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I Did

-   Designed and implemented **measurement systems** to capture
    mechanical, thermal, and electrical signals.\
-   Worked with sensors such as thermocouples, strain gauges, pressure
    transducers, and accelerometers.\
-   Used **Arduino microcontrollers** for rapid prototyping of sensor
    systems, integrating analog and digital signals into real-time data
    streams.\
-   Programmed Arduino boards to perform **signal conditioning, data
    logging, and control tasks** for lab experiments.\
-   Applied **calibration and error analysis** to improve accuracy and
    reliability of measurements.\
-   Built custom **DAQ (Data Acquisition)** setups combining Arduino
    with **MATLAB and LabVIEW** for real-time visualization and
    post-processing.\
-   **Trained computer vision models in MATLAB** (e.g., ACF and R-CNN)
    to detect objects such as stop signs --- building annotated
    datasets, training detectors, and testing for accuracy and false
    positives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Concepts in Action

-   **Physical Measurement:** Interfacing physical sensors with
    Arduino-based DAQ systems.\
-   **Data Acquisition (DAQ):** Using hardware/software pipelines to
    capture, digitize, and analyze sensor signals.\
-   **Computer Vision Models:** Training supervised models in MATLAB
    using labeled datasets for object detection tasks.\
-   **Model Evaluation:** Analyzing detector accuracy, recall/precision,
    and likelihood of false positives.\
-   **Integration:** Linking experimental DAQ systems with modern
    **image-based sensing**, showing how physical and digital
    instrumentation complement each other.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   Build **physical DAQ systems** using Arduino and professional DAQ
