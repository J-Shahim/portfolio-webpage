System Dynamics and Controls

System Dynamics and Controls is about understanding how systems behave
over time and learning how to guide that behavior through feedback and
control strategies. For me, this coursework was less about solving
equations in isolation and more about developing a toolkit to model,
simulate, and refine real-world engineering systems --- whether that's a
robotic arm, an aircraft stabilizer, or an energy device.

## What I Learned

-   Built mathematical models of mechanical, electrical, and
    electromechanical systems using differential equations, transfer
    functions, and state-space methods.\
-   Used symbolic tools in MATLAB to derive closed-form solutions and
    verify system behavior.\
-   Ran simulations in MATLAB/Simulink to visualize system response
    through step inputs, Bode plots, Nyquist diagrams, and root locus
    analysis.\
-   Designed and tuned controllers (PID, state-space feedback) to
    balance stability, precision, and robustness.

## Core Concepts in Practice

-   **Dynamic behavior:** How systems naturally respond to inputs and
    disturbances.\
-   **Controls strategies:**
    -   Open-loop for predictable systems.\
    -   Closed-loop (feedback) to automatically correct errors.\
    -   Feedforward to anticipate changes before they occur.\
-   **Design methods:** From classical tools like PID tuning and
    frequency response to modern state-space and optimal control for
    multi-variable systems.
