# Geothermal Ideal Rankine Cycle with Ammonia

This project analyzed the performance of an **ideal Rankine cycle**
using **ammonia as the working fluid**, applied to a **geothermal energy
system**. The goal was to deliver a **net power output of 5 MW** while
optimizing efficiency and reducing environmental heat pollution to a
downstream river acting as the low-temperature reservoir.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I Did

-   Designed an **ideal Rankine power cycle** with ammonia as the
    working fluid.\
-   Defined operating limits based on geothermal high-temperature and
    low-temperature reservoirs.\
-   Applied **isentropic assumptions** for the pump and turbine, with
    quality = 1 at turbine inlet and quality = 0 at pump inlet.\
-   Optimized cycle state points to balance efficiency and environmental
    impact.\
-   Used **EES (Engineering Equation Solver)** to calculate enthalpy,
    entropy, and state properties at each stage of the cycle.\
-   Verified results against **P-h and T-s diagrams** for ammonia.\
-   Optimized pressures: **Evaporator at 10.25 MPa** and **Condenser at
    0.675 MPa**.\
-   Determined cycle mass flow rate of **22.7 kg/s**, geothermal stream
    flow rate of **440 kg/s**, and river cooling flow rate of **1015
    kg/s**.\
-   Achieved **thermal cycle efficiency of 21.63%** and minimized river
    outlet temperature to **9.249 °C**, reducing environmental heat
    load.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Concepts in Action

-   **Rankine Cycle Thermodynamics:** Applying enthalpy balances across
    turbine, pump, condenser, and evaporator.\
-   **Working Fluid Selection:** Leveraging ammonia's thermophysical
    properties for efficient geothermal energy extraction.\
-   **Environmental Considerations:** Designing within heat rejection
    limits to minimize river temperature rise.\
-   **Heat Transfer Analysis:** Using UA limits for evaporator and
    condenser to model realistic system constraints.\
-   **Cycle Optimization:** Adjusting evaporator and condenser pressures
    to maximize efficiency while meeting reservoir constraints.
